About me

One person, from first sketch to launch day

I started in UI design and moved into WordPress development, so the same person draws the screen and writes the theme. There is no handoff in the middle where details get lost.

What I care about: layouts that are easy to read, pages that load quickly on a mid-range phone, and an admin area a client can actually use on Monday morning.

  • Design and build in one place

    The mockup and the theme come from the same hands, so nothing gets lost in translation.

  • Mobile first, always

    Every layout is tested from a 360px phone upward before it reaches a desktop screen.

  • Handed over properly

    You get a walkthrough of the admin and editable fields where your team needs them.

1

Discover

We agree on goals, the content you already have, and who the site is really for.

2

Design

Wireframes first, then mockups in Figma. Nothing gets built until you've signed off.

3

Build

A custom WordPress theme that matches the design, with editable fields where you need them.

4

Launch

Cross-browser testing, speed checks, and a walkthrough so your team can take it from there.
